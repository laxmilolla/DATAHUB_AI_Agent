--------------------------------

✅ They have implicit role, not the attribute!

<button aria-expanded="true">Text</button>  ← Won't match!
<div role="button" aria-expanded="true">Text</div>  ← Will match!

FIX: Change to:
button[aria-expanded], [role="button"][aria-expanded]
